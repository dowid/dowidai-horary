A PLAIN-LANGUAGE GUIDE

From your question
to a reading.

You ask a question. The app calculates a chart of the sky for that moment, and AI helps you explore its symbols as a starting point for reflection.

This is an experimental symbolic reading. It does not predict the future with demonstrated accuracy or establish how another person feels.

01 · YOUR QUESTION

Your situation, in your words.

Choose your own work or a relationship with a particular partner. Ask one specific question and select the city where you are now. You do not need your birth date. Leave out names, contact information and sensitive details.

The service records the time when it processes your request. A new request, even with the same wording, can therefore produce a different chart and explanation.

02 · THE CALCULATION

Software calculates the planetary positions.

We use Swiss Ephemeris, a calculation engine for celestial positions ↗. Using your city and the time, the application determines planetary positions and draws a chart.

The AI receives an already calculated chart; it does not work out where the planets are. Accurate astronomy does not establish that an interpretation can reliably predict your future.

03 · THE EXPLANATION

AI helps explain the result.

Readings currently use this OpenAI modelGPT-4.1 miniAbout the model ↗

The model receives your question, the calculated chart and a limited set of interpretation rules. It writes a short summary, up to three observations and a question for reflection in your selected language. It may ask you to clarify an unsuitable question.

We check that the answer refers to existing chart facts and permitted rules. This does not eliminate errors in the explanation. Compare the text with what you actually know about your situation.

04 · WHAT TO EXPECT

A perspective, without a final verdict.

This is an early version. Some traditional methods are not implemented and interpretations have not undergone expert review. See the technical limitations.

Readings should not promise exact dates, probabilities or definitive yes/no answers. They do not replace a conversation, evidence or professional support. Health, investments, legal disputes and other people's secrets are outside the service's scope.

The beta allows up to 10 reading attempts per browser in a rolling 24-hour period, with a one-minute pause between requests. A shared monthly budget also applies.

05 · YOUR ARCHIVE

Return to a question when you need to.

My questions stores your readings in this browser's private archive. Clearing the site's cookie or switching devices loses access; recovery is not yet available. Use Download reading to keep important results and download the chart as PNG or SVG.

Share creates a link to one reading, including the question and city. Anyone who has it can open it. Revoke link removes that access; Delete removes the record from the live database. Copies already downloaded by someone else cannot be recalled.

Your question and chart are sent to OpenAI to generate the explanation. We keep the question, city, time, chart and answer until you delete them or the service ends. Provider backups can remain longer. The owner sees aggregate statistics, not question text in the dashboard.

You can say it aloud.

Choose Ask by voice, consent to sending the recording to OpenAI and allow microphone access. The bars respond to your voice. Record up to 30 seconds, stop, then review and edit the transcript. Your question is only submitted when you press Get a reading. Horary does not store audio. Voice input has a separate allowance of 10 attempts per 24 hours and uses the same shared monthly budget.

Two languages, one archive.

Choose English or Русский from the language menu. New readings and voice transcription use that language. Saved answers retain their original language; switching the interface does not rewrite them.
