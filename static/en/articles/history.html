A tradition concerned with practical questions

Horary belongs to the wider history of astrological divination. Its distinctive focus is a question about a concrete situation rather than a general portrait of a person. Relationships, work and missing objects are familiar topics in the tradition.

William Lilly and Christian Astrology

The English astrologer William Lilly published Christian Astrology in 1647. It became an influential English-language source on traditional astrology, including detailed horary examples. Its historical importance does not establish that its judgements are reliable predictions. William Lilly at Skyscript.

Why people still study it

A defined question, a visible chart and a structured interpretation make the method attractive to people interested in traditional astrology. Modern schools and authors continue to teach it. Different practitioners can disagree about rules and how much weight each factor deserves.

What we take from this history

The app makes the calculation and its limited rules visible. It does not claim to reproduce a complete historical consultation. Expert evaluation and documented reference cases are necessary before making stronger claims about the implementation.
