Where astronomical calculations end and interpretations begin.

Planetary positions are calculable

Coordinates, speeds and house boundaries can be reproduced using the same time, location and settings. Our engine is Swiss Ephemeris. Agreement on coordinates is evidence about the calculation, not about the meaning assigned to it.

Interpretation is a different claim

An accurate position does not demonstrate that it predicts a relationship or job outcome. Those claims need independent tests with clear criteria, comparison groups and outcomes assessed without hindsight. Personal impressions and memorable examples alone cannot settle the question.

Research needs careful boundaries

Shawn Carlson’s 1985 double-blind test in Nature examined claims about natal astrology and personality. It is relevant to discussions of astrological evidence, but it is not a direct test of every horary rule or this application. Read the study.

How to use this product

Treat the reading as symbolic material for reflection. Compare it with facts and conversations. The chart, model name, rules and limitations are visible so you can inspect what the app actually did. A helpful-sounding answer is not proof of a prediction, and an AI explanation can be wrong.
